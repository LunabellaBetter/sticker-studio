# Example · Ten-sticker expression brief

This is an example of turning a user-confirmed phrase list into a production-ready brief. The actual interpretation must follow the user's context and feedback.

| ID | Caption | Intended meaning | Facial acting | Body / gesture | Motion arc |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ST-01 | 无语 | Speechless, exasperated, too tired to explain | half-lidded eyes, tiny frown, slow blink | hands support cheeks | glance → slow blink → sigh → hold |
| ST-02 | 哼！ | Playful defiance / pout | closed eyes, puckered mouth | arms cross, head turns away | inhale → cross arms → turn → hold |
| ST-03 | what?! | Sudden surprise | eyes widen, brows rise, mouth opens | hands lift toward cheeks, slight recoil | notice → recoil → hands up → hold |
| ST-04 | 哇哇大哭 | Overwhelmed crying | brows arch upward, eyes squeeze, mouth opens | shoulders shake, hands wipe tears | tremble → tears flow → sob → settle |
| ST-05 | 嘿嘿 | Mischievous, pleased with oneself | side glance, squinting grin | hand partly covers mouth | peek → grin grows → tiny shoulder bounce |
| ST-06 | 嘻嘻 | Bright, genuine giggle | crescent eyes, open smile, lifted cheeks | small happy bounce or hands near chest | smile builds → giggle → hold |
| ST-07 | 不嘻嘻 | The smile disappears; suddenly serious / disappointed | smile fades, eyes flatten, brows lower | body stills, hands settle | smile → freeze → expression drops → hold |
| ST-08 | 超棒！ | Excited approval / encouragement | bright eyes, broad smile | fist pump or clear thumbs-up | anticipate → raise fist / thumb → cheer → hold |
| ST-09 | 爱你！ | Warm affection | smiling eyes, soft smile | form a heart with both hands | hands rise → heart forms → hold |
| ST-10 | 碎觉觉 | Sleepy, ready to go to bed | drooping eyelids, small yawn | rub eyes, hug pillow | yawn → rub eyes → cuddle → drift |

## Production constraints

- Each row becomes its own independent sticker file.
- A contact sheet may be used for review, never as the final deliverable.
- Choose static PNG or GIF per expression; use GIF when the motion adds meaning.
- Keep the character identity fixed across all ten.
- Use the high-resolution and scale targets in `docs/09-resolution-and-framing.md`.
- Apply the GIF pass/fail checklist in `docs/08-gif-approval-rules.md`.
